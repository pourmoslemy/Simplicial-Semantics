## 📌 Overview

This repository contains the author's original manuscript (preprint) and supplementary formal derivations for:

> **Simplicial Semantics for Intuitionistic Logic: A No-Go Theorem and Geometric Intermediate Logics**  
> **Author:** Alireza Pourmoslemi  
> **Current Status:** Submitted and under peer review at *Annals of Pure and Applied Logic (APAL)*.

This work establishes the geometric and topological foundations of **Simplicial Intuitionistic Logic (SC-Logic)**, bridging categorical logic, combinatorial algebraic topology, and Heyting algebras.

---

## 🔬 Key Contributions

1. **Simplicial Realization of Heyting Semantics:**  
   Construction of geometric valuation spaces over simplicial complexes and Kan complexes, establishing an antitonic graded negation $\neg_\Delta$ compatible with simplicial filtrations.
   
2. **Algebraic No-Go Theorem:**  
   Proof of a fundamental obstruction theorem demonstrating why classic graph-theoretic (dyadic) abstractions cannot preserve higher-order intuitionistic consistency without degenerating to Boolean triviality.

3. **Geometric Intermediate Logics:**  
   Characterization of a continuum of intermediate logics parameterized by topological dimension and homology spheres ($H_k(K)$).

4. **Applications:**  
   Serves as the rigorous mathematical engine underlying geometric polarization metrics, multi-agent consensus protocols, and topological decision intelligence (VeriLogos).
